- **The stock and the reports now feed the change log of the mobile app.** Every write of a stock item, a supplier, a stock movement, the rooms and compatible items of a stock item, and a report adds an entry to the change log, for the fields that changed. Nothing reads the log yet. The nightly purge also removes, with a stock item or a report that it deletes for good, the entries of its suppliers, movements and links and the photo files that would otherwise stay on the disk. See [Data model of the synchronization](../../../developer/sync-data-model.md).
