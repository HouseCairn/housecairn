- **Filters in tabs, search as you type, one target a row.** A filter of state is now a tab, with the current one marked: **Open**, **Closed**, **All** and **Mine** on the [reports](../../../screens/reports.md), **To do** and **Done** on the [tasks](../../../screens/tasks.md), **All** and **Low stock** on the [stock](../../../screens/stock.md). Several criteria are folded under **Filters (2)**, a sheet that says how many apply, on the [projects](../../../screens/projects.md), the [history](../../../screens/history.md) and the [equipment](../../../screens/equipment.md). The search fields have a magnifier and no button: the list follows what you type, after a short pause, and the address keeps the words. The [rooms](../../../screens/rooms.md) search shows its results in place. The year of the [budget](../../../screens/budget.md) follows its choice, without **Show**, and the repeated **By room**, **By project** and **By task** links are gone from its summary. A row is one link over its whole width: the tasks of a [project](../../../screens/project.md) no longer carry a **Take out** button (it is on the page of the task), a budget line opens its form from its name, and the rows of the budget tables open their room, project or task from anywhere. **Compare projects** turns the rows into boxes to tick. The two forms for a project's tasks are two buttons. Without JavaScript, everything still works: Enter sends a search, and a **Filter** or **Show** button appears where a choice needs one.
