- **Finishing touches of the screens.** Each entry of [More](../../../screens/more.md) has its icon. The page of [task templates](../../../screens/task-templates.md) is lists of rows under a heading per equipment, each row one box to tick, and it can be opened from a [room](../../../screens/room.md), with that room already chosen. An [item](../../../screens/item.md) with no photo shows a dashed frame under **Photos**, and the actions of a room and of an item are one row of buttons as wide as their text. The budget lines of a [project](../../../screens/project.md) name the planned amount with the same word as the other budget screens (in French, *Prévu* everywhere instead of *Prévue* on this one). On the sign-in page, each language is a button: tapping it applies it, and the **Change** button is gone.
