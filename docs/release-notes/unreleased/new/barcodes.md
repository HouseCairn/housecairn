- **Barcodes.** A stock item and an item of a room keep their barcode (EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A or GTIN-14, with its check digit), which you can type or scan with the camera, and you can search by it. Scanning a barcode can fill in the empty fields of the form from a local copy of the open Nudger catalogue and, if the administrator turns it on, from Open Products Facts and Open Food Facts. Both are off by default.
