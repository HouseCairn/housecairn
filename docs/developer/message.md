# Message

The one way HouseCairn describes itself. The website, the README of every
repository, this documentation and the GitHub descriptions reuse these words, so
that a newcomer reads the same thing everywhere. Change the message here first,
then everywhere it is quoted, in the same round of pull requests.

## Headline

Plan your home's maintenance and renovation work, room by room.

## Paragraph

HouseCairn keeps the list of everything your house needs, from recurring upkeep
such as the smoke detector battery to projects such as repainting the living
room. For each job it knows the room, the date, the supplies and the planned
cost, and anyone in the household marks it done in one tap. It is free software
that you host yourself, works on its own, and connects to Home Assistant if you
use it.

## Examples

Real situations, each covered by what exists on the `main` branch:

- **The smoke detector**: test it every six months and change its battery when
  it is due, without having to remember the last time.
- **The ventilation filter**: clean it every three months, and get a warning
  when the spare filters in stock run low.
- **The boiler**: its yearly service, with the brand, the model and the end of
  the warranty kept on the boiler's page.
- **Repainting the living room**: a project with its quotes and its budget
  lines (paint and primer, the painter for the ceiling), planned, committed and
  paid, and the paint reference kept on the room for next time.
- **The gutters**: cleared every autumn only, as a seasonal task.

## Who it is for, and what it is not

HouseCairn is for the people who live in a house or a flat, owners or tenants,
alone or as a household, who want one shared list instead of notes,
spreadsheets and memory.

It is not:

- **business maintenance software** (a CMMS) for buildings, sites or fleets;
- **a home automation app**: it drives no device and reads no sensor by itself.
  It works alongside [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/), which
  calls HouseCairn, never the other way round;
- **a home inventory** of everything you own, nor a grocery and household
  manager: [Homebox](https://homebox.software/) and
  [Grocy](https://grocy.info/) do that well. HouseCairn keeps only the items
  that need upkeep (the boiler, the smoke detectors) and the supplies they use.
