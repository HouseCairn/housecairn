# Tasks

The tasks of a home, at `/h/<home id>/tasks/`: the **All** tab of **Today** in the
navigation. `/tasks/` opens it directly, on the
[current home](my-home.md#the-current-home) when you belong to several.

Each row is one time a task has to be done (an occurrence): its title, the room
and the item it concerns, and when. The oldest dates come first, so the arrears
stay on top and are shown in red (**2 days late**); a task with no date comes
last. A task made from a report that has a photo carries a **Photo** label next
to its date (a read-only member sees it for their own reports only). A row opens
the [task](task.md); **Done** on the right of the row marks it done in one tap,
for every task, without reloading the page, and a banner at the bottom offers
**Undo** right after, as on [Today](today.md).

- The first selector switches between **To do** and **Done**. **Done** is the
  [history](history.md): what was done or skipped, the most recent first, with its
  filters and its real cost. With **To do**, the second selector keeps the tasks of
  one room.
- A task can be hidden until a few days before its date (see
  [Tasks and recurrence](../guides/tasks-and-recurrence.md)). The page tells how
  many are hidden and **Show them** lists them anyway.
- The list shows 30 rows at a time.
- The **tabs** above the selectors are those of [Today](today.md): **Today**, **All**
  and **Calendar**. The [contractors](contractors.md) and the
  [display preferences](display-preferences.md) are in [More](more.md).
- **Add a task** opens the [task form](task-form.md).
- **Add from a template** opens the [library of task templates](task-templates.md).

Read-only members see the same list and none of the buttons that change it.
