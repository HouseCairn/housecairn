# About

The version of the application (for example `Version 0.4.1`, the `version` of
`pyproject.toml`, selectable to copy it into a report), the licence (AGPL-3.0), a
link to the source code and security reports, and the statement that HouseCairn is
developed with an AI agent under human review. It is at `/about/` and does not need
an account.

Under **Open data**, the open databases the [barcode lookup](../administration/barcodes.md)
reads (Open Products Facts, then Open Food Facts) and their licences: the Open Database
License (ODbL) for their data, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 for the photos.

Under **Links**, a row of icons with their label leads to the project website, the
documentation, the application code, the Home Assistant app, the Home Assistant integration
and the mobile application, each on its own site. **iOS app (TestFlight beta)** opens the
page to join the beta in a new tab (iPhone, TestFlight required). The **Android** icon is
marked **Coming soon** and is not a link: the application is not published in a store
yet. The icons are drawn in the page; nothing is loaded from those sites until you
follow a link.

Once signed in, the link at the top leads back to [More](more.md), on a phone.
