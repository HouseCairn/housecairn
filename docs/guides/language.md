# Language

HouseCairn speaks English and French. Each person chooses their own language; the other
members of a home keep theirs.

## Choose your language

Open **More**, then **Display preferences** (also reached from **Profile**, **Change the
language**), and pick **Language**:

- **Automatic**: the language of your browser, if HouseCairn ships it, else the default
  language of the instance, set by its administrator.
- **English** or **Français**: that language, whatever your browser says, on every device
  you sign in from.

**Save** applies it at once: the message that confirms it, and every page after, are in the
new language.

## Where it applies

Everything you read follows the same choice:

- every screen, the administration site, the help links (they open the documentation in your
  language) and the dates, numbers and amounts;
- the pages kept for reading offline: after a change, the pages kept in the former language
  are dropped, and the offline page is in your language;
- what HouseCairn sends you: reminders, the daily summary, notifications on your devices,
  the test message of a channel and your calendar feeds;
- the API, with a token as with the session: your choice wins over the `Accept-Language`
  of the call.

With **Automatic**, what is sent to you has no browser to ask: it is in the default language
of the instance.

## Before signing in

The sign-in and sign-up pages end with **Language** and **Change**: the choice is kept in a
cookie of your browser until you sign in, then your own choice applies. A language chosen in
your display preferences is also written to that cookie, so the sign-in page speaks it after
you sign out.
